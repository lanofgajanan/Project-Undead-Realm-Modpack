# THE UNDEAD REALM — Player Guide

## Getting Started

When you spawn into the world, you'll receive a basic **Drifter Kit** (wooden tools, bread). Open the **Skills Menu** by pressing **K** to choose your class and start building your character.

---

## Time & Day Cycle

The day/night cycle in The Undead Realm is **not standard Minecraft**.

| Phase | Duration | Total |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Daytime | 30 minutes (real time) | |
| Nighttime | 30 minutes (real time) | |
| **Full Day** | **1 hour (real time)** | 72,000 ticks |

Nights are just as long as days. Prepare accordingly — you'll spend half your time in darkness. Stock up on ammo, torches, and food before sunset.

## Seasons (Ecliptic Seasons)

The world has a **full 4-season cycle** that affects gameplay:

### Season Calendar

| Season | Duration | Effect |
|--------|----------|--------|
| **Spring** | 18 MC days (~18 hours) | Normal days, crops grow normally |
| **Summer** | 18 MC days (~18 hours) | Longer days, shorter nights. Heatstroke in hot biomes at noon |
| **Autumn** | 18 MC days (~18 hours) | Days get shorter, leaves change |
| **Winter** | 18 MC days (~18 hours) | Short days, long nights. Snow covers the world |

Each season has 6 Solar Terms (3 MC days each). You'll get a chat notification when the term changes.

### How Seasons Affect You

- **Crops**: Plants only grow in their compatible seasons. Bone meal can fail if the season is wrong
- **Daylight**: Summer has up to 15,000 ticks of daylight, Winter drops to 9,000
- **Snow**: Visual snow covers blocks in Winter, even in normally warm biomes
- **Weather**: Rain is more frequent in certain seasons
- **Greenhouses**: Build enclosed glass structures to grow crops year-round

### Building a Greenhouse

If you want crops in the wrong season, build a greenhouse:
- Enclosed glass/solid block structure (max 32x32x10)
- Use a **Humidifier** block to control internal humidity
- Use a **Season Core** block (15-block radius) to override seasonal restrictions

## Temperature & Thirst (Tough As Nails)

### Thirst

You have a **thirst bar** alongside hunger. It depletes as you move and fight.

- **Hand drinking**: Right-click water sources to drink (restores 3 thirst)
- **Canteen**: Craftable container to carry water
- **When thirsty**: You can't sprint, and health won't regenerate
- Purified water is better — dirty water can give negative effects

### Temperature

Your body temperature matters. Extreme heat or cold will damage you.

- **Biome-based**: Deserts are hot, tundras are cold
- **Altitude**: High up = colder, deep underground = warmer
- **Season-linked**: Winter makes everything colder, Summer adds heatstroke
- **Armor**: Wearing armor affects temperature (leather = warm, iron = cold)
- **Climate Clemency**: After respawning, you get ~15 minutes of temperature immunity

**Tips:**
- Carry a campfire or torch for warmth in Winter
- Don't wear heavy armor in the Desert during Summer
- Build shelter — being indoors moderates temperature

---

## Food Spoiling

Food has a **spoil timer**. Raw meat and cooked food will rot over time. Check item tooltips for spoil time remaining.

- **Cooked food**: Lasts longer than raw
- **Dried/preserved food**: Lasts the longest (Farmers Delight recipes)
- **Storage**: Keep food in cold areas to slow spoiling (ice nearby helps)

## Guns & Combat (TACZ)

### Gun Basics

| Action | Key |
|--------|-----|

| Fire | Left Click |
| Reload | R |
| Switch Fire Mode | B (SEMI/AUTO/BURST) |

### Getting Guns

There are several ways to obtain firearms:

1. **Class starter kit** — your first gun, given when you choose a class
2. **Loot chests** — guns spawn in structure chests (tiered by difficulty)
3. **Villager trades** — Weaponsmiths sell guns and ammo, Armorers sell attachments
4. **Wandering Trader** — sells ammo (always), rare guns, and vehicle crates
5. **Gun Smith Table** — craft guns using materials at the TACZ workbench

### Loot Chest Tiers

| Chest Location | What You'll Find |
|----------------|-----------------|
| **Villages, Mineshafts** | Pistols, shotguns, basic ammo, grips, red dot sights |
| **Dungeons, Pyramids, Temples** | SMGs, more ammo types, scopes, stocks, ext mags |
| **Strongholds, Mansions, End Cities** | Rifles (40% chance), silencers, ammo mods (ultra rare) |

### Ammo in Loot

Ammo always spawns alongside guns. Higher tier chests give more and better ammo. Common ammo types in loot: 9mm, .45 ACP, 12g shells, .357 Mag, .308, 5.56x45.

### Attachments

Find or buy attachments to upgrade your guns:

| Tier | Examples | Where to Find |
|------|----------|---------------|
| Common | Grips, red dots (Coyote, T1, T2) | Village chests, Armorer Lv1-2 |
| Uncommon | ACOG, LPVO scope, stocks, ext mags | Dungeon chests, Armorer Lv3 |
| Rare | Silencers (Wraith, Phantom, etc.) | Stronghold chests, Armorer Lv4 |
| Ultra Rare | Ammo mods (FMJ, HP, HE, Incendiary, Slug) | End city chests, Armorer Lv5 |

Land vehicles are available from the Wandering Trader or crafted at the Vehicle Assembly Table.

## Pillager Raids

Pillagers in The Undead Realm carry **TACZ firearms** instead of crossbows. They're armed with:
- Pistols (Glock, M1911, P320, Taurus, Rhino)
- Shotguns (DB Short, DB Long, M870)
- SMGs (UZI)
- Rifles (AK47, RPK, Kar98)
- Lever/Bolt actions (Springfield 1873)

They will NOT carry high-tier weapons like snipers, SCARs, or explosives. Still dangerous in groups — take cover and use range to your advantage.

## Saturday Blood Moon Siege

**Every real-life Saturday between 6 PM and 9 PM**, a special event triggers:

### The Raid Sequence

| MC Night | Event | Difficulty |
|----------|-------|------------|
| 1 | **Spore Raid** — Mid-tier infected swarm (Howler, Knight, Brute, Slasher) | Medium |
| 2 | **Spore Raid** — Hard-tier infected assault (Griefer, Volatile, Leaper, Stalker, Bloater) | Hard |
| 3 | **Pillager Raid** — Full vanilla raid triggered at your location | Hard |
| 4 | **Pillager Raid** — Another full raid, then siege ends | Hard |

### Tips for Blood Moon

- Spore and Pillager raids NEVER happen on the same night (no friendly fire chaos)
- Build defenses before Saturday — walls, turrets, chokepoints
- Stock up on ammo and healing items
- The siege only activates if you're online during the window
- You'll see red chat messages announcing each wave

## Gun Penetration System

Not all guns can damage all enemies. The Spore infection creates biological armor that resists weak firearms.

### How It Works

TACZ guns have an **armor_ignore** stat that determines penetration. Mobs with high armor reduce incoming bullet damage. If the result is too low, the bullet is **deflected** (zero damage).

You'll see a red **[DEFLECTED]** message in chat when your weapon can't penetrate.

### What Penetrates What

| Enemy Tier | Examples | What Works | What Doesn't |
|-----------|----------|------------|--------------|
| **Fodder** (Tier 0-1) | Infected humans, Howlers | Everything | Nothing blocked |
| **Hyper Evolved** (Tier 2) | Brute, Ogre, Wendigo | Rifles, SMGs, high-cal pistols | Glock, M9A4, CZ75 |
| **Experiments** (Tier 3) | Proto Hivemind, Construct | Battle rifles, snipers, AP pistols | Basic SMGs, weak rifles |
| **Calamities** (Tier 4) | Sieger, Hohlfresser, Leviathan | Snipers, battle rifles, P90, Deagle+, FMJ ammo | Most pistols, SMGs, assault rifles |

### Tips for Fighting Tough Enemies

- **Use FMJ ammo** — equip the FMJ attachment to boost penetration (lets rifles damage Calamities)
- **Snipers always work** — M700, Kar98, AWP, M95, M107 penetrate everything
- **Battle rifles are reliable** — SCAR-H, HK G3, FN FAL, MK14 handle most threats
- **P90 is special** — its 5.7x28mm round has exceptional armor penetration
- **Melee ALWAYS works** — regardless of gun penetration, melee weapons damage everything with bonus multipliers
- **Explosives bypass everything** — RPG-7, M320, TNT ignore armor entirely

### Melee Bonus Damage

Melee weapons get bonus damage against all Spore infected:

|--------|-----------|
| Netherite Axe | 2.5x |

## Class System

### Choosing a Class

1. Press **K** to open the skills menu
2. Go to the **"Choose Class"** tab
3. Click on one of the 8 class icons to select your specialization
4. **This choice is permanent** — you cannot change class later

When you pick a class:
- You receive a **starter kit** with class-specific weapons and gear
- A new tab unlocks with your class's full skill tree (70 skills)
- Other class tabs remain locked forever

### The 8 Classes

| Class | Playstyle | Starter Weapon | Specialty |
|-------|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| **Gunner** | Ranged DPS | M700 Sniper + Glock | TACZ gun damage, recoil, reload |
| **Brawler** | Melee Tank | Shotgun + Iron Sword | Attack damage, attack speed |
| **Technician** | Builder/Miner | Uzi + Iron Pickaxe | Block break speed, luck |
| **Medic** | Healer/Tank | MP5 + Golden Apples | Max health (heavy), knockback resist |
| **Ghost** | Stealth/Speed | M1911 + Iron Dagger | Movement speed, attack speed |
| **Chemist** | Poison/DoT | Rhino 357 + Brewing Stand | Attack damage, luck |
| **Recon** | Mobile Sniper | M700 + Uzi + Elytra | ADS damage, gun movement speed |
| **Breacher** | Heavy Assault | Shotgun + M1911 + TNT | Max health, knockback resist, armor |

## Skill Trees

### How Skills Work

- Each class has **10 skill paths** with 7 nodes each
- Every path branches: L1 → choose L2a OR L2b → then L3 from your choice
- **You have 21 skill points max** — enough for 7 full paths (out of 10)
- Choose wisely — you can't unlock everything

### Earning Skill Points

Kill mobs to earn XP. Every mob drops skill XP equal to **3x its vanilla XP drop**.

| Level | XP Needed | Approximate Kills |
|-------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | 55 | ~4 zombies |
| 5 | 304 | ~20 zombies |
| 10 | 718 | ~48 zombies |
| 15 | 1203 | ~80 zombies |
| 21 (max) | 1500 | ~100 zombies |

Stronger mobs give more XP. Ender Dragon, Wither, and Spore Calamities give massive XP.

### Drifter Tab

Everyone has access to the **Drifter** tab (18 points, 6 paths). These are general survival buffs that work regardless of class — health, speed, damage, knockback resist, luck, and mining.

## Survival Tips

1. **Pick a class early** — the starter kit helps enormously in early game
2. **Manage thirst** — carry a canteen or stay near water sources
3. **Watch the seasons** — crops die in wrong seasons, build greenhouses for reliability
4. **Prepare for Winter** — shorter days, longer nights, colder temperatures
5. **Melee is viable** — Brawler class with a good axe can one-shot most infected
6. **Save sniper ammo for bosses** — don't waste .308 on fodder
7. **FMJ ammo is essential** — equip it before fighting Experiments or Calamities
8. **The Drifter tree is free XP** — invest early for general stat boosts
9. **Explosives are rare but powerful** — RPGs bypass all armor, save them for emergencies
10. **Trade with villagers** — Weaponsmiths are your best ammo source mid-game
11. **Saturday night is dangerous** — be prepared between 6-9 PM for blood moon raids
12. **Vehicles save time** — buy a pickup from the wandering trader for long trips
13. **Food rots** — cook and eat promptly, or preserve food with Farmers Delight recipes
